LMS Guardian

Paste your extension code. You get it from the LMS Guardian panel (People tab), or from the person who added you to the bot. It stays in this browser only.

Telegram alerts for SIS marks (owner only)

Only the bot's owner fills this in: the same GitHub key and data passphrase as the panel. Then new SIS marks are also sent to your Telegram. Leave empty otherwise.

Connect my own Moodle (optional)

The bot follows its owner's subjects by default. If your subjects are different, get your personal Moodle token here and send it to the bot as /connect TOKEN. Your Moodle email and password are used once, right here in your browser, to ask Moodle for the token. They are never saved or sent anywhere else. The token only lets the bot read your subjects, and you can remove it any time with /disconnect.
